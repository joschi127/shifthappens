# Coding Conventions & Rules for Aider

## Core Principles
- **Surgical Precision:** Make the minimum number of changes required to accomplish the task. Do not rewrite whole files or large blocks if a small tweak is enough.
- **No Unsolicited Refactoring:** Do not refactor, clean up, or modernize existing code, variables, or functions unless it is explicitly requested by the user.
- **Preserve Existing Style:** Follow the formatting, indentation, naming conventions, and style of the surrounding code. Do not reformat untouched parts of the file.
- **Keep Comments Intact:** Do not remove, alter, or rewrite existing comments, docstrings, or documentation unless they are directly affected by the requested logic change.
- **No Over-Engineering:** Stick strictly to the prompt. Do not add unrequested features, extra error handling, or speculative optimizations.

## Search / Replace Blocks
- Keep `SEARCH` blocks as short and concise as possible.
- Include only the lines that actually need to be changed, plus just enough surrounding context lines to ensure unique identification.
